Product design · 10 screens · revision 5

Turn the group chat into a plan everyone agreed to.

Rebuilt flat. No gradients, no glows, no colored shadows — surfaces are white, separation comes from hairline rules, and the only saturated colour in the interface is on things you can act on. Navy carries hierarchy, blue means "the group wants this." The consensus meter is still the signature: one segment per person, so agreement is something you read rather than argue about.
